Minecraft x GTA V: Clean Install, on Melty
A remix of "Minecraft x GTA V" by Melty (https://melty.gg/m/minecraft-x-gta-v), v0.1.5. The game is the same;
only how it installs changed:
- No dinput8.dll of its own: Melty installs and manages Ultimate ASI Loader, which loads MCPassthrough.asi and
  ReShade64.asi.
- No args.txt in GTA V's folder: BattlEye is turned off (-nobattleye -noBE) only when Melty starts GTA V for this
  mashup, so other launches of GTA V are left alone.
- A smaller Minecraft bundle: Prism Launcher's self-updater and four unused image plugins are left out.
Story mode only.

The original's credits follow, unchanged.

Changes from that commit, for Melty:
- During a story mission the plugin steps aside, so the mission plays as plain GTA (its weapons, aiming, hints and
  police) and can be finished; Steve comes back in free roam when it ends. A new game opens with two missions (the
  Prologue, then Franklin and Lamar), so Steve first shows after those.
- When GTA directs the scene (a cutscene, a scripted camera, no player control, a fade, a character switch), GTA's
  own character acts it and Minecraft isn't drawn over it; Steve comes back with the player's control.
- GTA's player is hidden again whenever GTA has shown them, and a character left behind after a switch shows again.
